Your own music, in high quality, without filling up your phone.

MyMusic plays the music you have downloaded legally or that is free of copyright — from your iPhone, iCloud Drive or Google Drive — in its original quality, without having to store it all on your phone.

Everything you need to enjoy your music

☁ Play from the cloud

Connect iCloud Drive or Google Drive and listen without downloading your whole library. A smart cache makes replays instant.

♫ High quality, untouched

MP3, AAC/M4A and FLAC files are played as they are — no re-encoding, no compression on top.

▤ Albums & playlists

Browse by albums, artists and songs. Build playlists, keep your liked songs and manage the queue.

⇩ Offline when you need it

Save the songs you choose for flights and the metro, with a Wi-Fi-only option and a cache limit you control.

◉ Bluetooth & AirPlay

Send the sound to headphones, Bluetooth speakers or AirPlay speakers, and control it from the lock screen.

🔒 Private by design

No account with us, no ads, no tracking. Your music and library stay on your device and in your own cloud.

Music is not included. MyMusic does not include, sell or distribute music. It is a player for files you add yourself. Use only music you are entitled to use — music you bought and downloaded, or recordings in the public domain or free of copyright, such as the classical recordings from Musopen used in our guide.
