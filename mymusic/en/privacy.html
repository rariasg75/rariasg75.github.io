Privacy policy – MyMusic

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short

MyMusic does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal data. We have no servers that receive your information, no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Your music, your library and your settings stay on your device and in your own cloud storage.

Who we are

MyMusic is developed by [Developer name] (“we”). You can contact us at support@mymusic.example.

What the app stores on your device

To work, MyMusic keeps the following only on your iPhone: the list of folders you chose; information read from your music files (title, artist, album, cover art, duration); your playlists, liked songs and listening queue; your settings; a cache of streamed songs; and songs you chose to download for offline listening. This information never leaves your device through MyMusic. Deleting the app deletes it.

Files, iCloud Drive and Google Drive

Files and iCloud Drive. MyMusic can only access the folders you select, using the standard iOS file picker. It reads your music; it does not modify or delete it.

Google Drive. If you choose to connect Google Drive, you sign in with Google. MyMusic requests read-only access to your Drive files so it can list the audio files in the folder you pick, read their tags and play them. The audio data and file information go directly from Google to your device; they are not sent to us or to any third party. Your Google sign-in credentials are stored securely on your device, and you can disconnect at any time with Settings ▸ Sign out or by removing MyMusic at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

MyMusic’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the playback features described here; we do not transfer it to others, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, device identifiers or usage data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services that collect data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.
  • We do not host, provide or scan the content of your music beyond reading its tags to build your library.

Apple and Google

Apple (iOS, iCloud, the App Store) and Google (Google Drive) process data under their own privacy policies when you use their services. MyMusic does not control that processing.

Your rights and choices

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your device: clear the cache, remove downloads or sign out in Settings, or delete the app to remove all local data. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or elsewhere with data-protection rights, you may contact us with any question about them.

Children

MyMusic is a general-audience music player and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Security

Data stays on your device, protected by iOS. Sign-in credentials for Google Drive are kept in secure storage on your device and are used only to talk to Google.

Changes to this policy

If we change how MyMusic handles data, we will update this page and the date above, and, for significant changes, inform you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@mymusic.example.
